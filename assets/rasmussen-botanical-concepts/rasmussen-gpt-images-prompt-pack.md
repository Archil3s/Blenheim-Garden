# Rasmussen Gardens icon prompts for GPT Images

285 individual outputs from the supplied catalogue, including six seed collections.

## Uploads for each generation

1. Cultivar reference. Upload a clear photo of the exact named variety. Use the product link under each prompt to find a reference, or search for the full cultivar name and verify the source. If available, add a second photo that shows a different angle or a defining feature.
2. Style references. Upload the same two or three isolated, approved planner icons for each generation. Good examples are the detailed Kings Seeds asparagus, purple sprouting broccoli and Carolina Reaper icons. Keep these separate from the cultivar photo.
3. For a seed collection, use its verified contents or product photo as reference. Draw a seed packet or a small assortment of the verified contents.

## Master prompt

Create one original botanical icon for a garden planner. The first uploaded image shows the cultivar and controls the real shape, proportions, surface and colour. The other uploaded images show the illustration style only. Match their painterly shading, fine contours, organic shapes and detail. Preserve the cultivar's distinguishing visible traits. Show it from a clear three-quarter angle, with overlapping parts separated by soft shadows. Keep the full silhouette centred on a square canvas with roughly 8–10% padding and a transparent background. Make the plant readable at 32 pixels. Use no text, border, soil, pot, watermark, or ground shadow. Return one standalone image.

Paste the relevant item prompt below after the master prompt. Review each result at 32 px before accepting it. These links are starting points for finding photos. Photos are not embedded or individually verified in this pack.
